# Code of conduct

Little Orbit welcomes contributors of every background, identity, relationship structure, and experience level. Be kind, specific, and patient. Critique work and decisions without attacking people. Do not harass, sexualize, threaten, expose private information, or pressure anyone to discuss their relationship or identity.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled with care and shared only with people needed to resolve them.

This project serves adults, but contribution spaces must remain suitable for general professional collaboration. Product discussions about optional intimacy prompts must stay non-graphic and consent-centered.
