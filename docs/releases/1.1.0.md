# Little Orbit 1.1.0

Little Orbit 1.1.0 is the signed watch update. It keeps watch installation,
updates, removal, content choices, and diagnostics together while preserving an
explicit person action before any APK download or installation.

## Phone watch settings

- Adds a dedicated Watch settings page with one explicitly selected managed
  watch, content-free connection and version status, and private diagnostics.
- Controls profile photos, countdown titles, watch Smooches, metadata-only
  update alerts, and the default Together, Countdown, or Smooch destination.
- Adds an explicit five-step installer for install/update and repair. Opening
  the wizard never downloads APK bytes by itself.
- Private removal first advances the durable watch-target purge barrier, then
  clears queued actions, uninstalls only Little Orbit, forgets the local
  installer key, and explains how to revoke wireless debugging on the watch.

## Wear OS destinations

- Replaces the horizontal launcher flow with vertically scrolling Together,
  Countdown, and Smooch destinations designed for round displays.
- Preserves timed-instant versus all-day local-date countdown semantics and the
  countdown's IANA timezone.
- Restricts Smooch to approved choices and requires a confirmation screen. The
  watch keeps at most five AES-GCM-protected requests for at most 15 minutes.
- Improves the passive tile and provides separate Nearby and Countdown
  complications. Passive surfaces never send a Smooch.

## Privacy and mixed versions

- Every active watch record carries the opaque relationship identity plus
  monotonic relationship and watch-target generations. Switching or removing
  a watch advances the target purge before the old target may receive data.
- Watch-originated actions carry no account token. The phone revalidates the
  selected node, protocol, target and relationship generations, preference,
  approved emoji, identifier, and creation window before its authenticated
  outbox accepts ownership.
- A 1.1.0 phone safely identifies and guides replacement of an older companion;
  an older phone cannot activate 1.1.0-only watch actions.
