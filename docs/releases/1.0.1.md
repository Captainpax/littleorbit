# Little Orbit 1.0.1

Little Orbit 1.0.1 is the signed reliability update for estimated nearby time
and the shared Our Space editor.

## Together Time

- Counts direct nearby evidence within five minutes and may later bridge a gap
  of at most 20 minutes only when a second strong nearby anchor confirms it and
  no intervening observation reports apart or poor accuracy.
- Keeps an open gap out of the durable total, records confirmed bridges
  separately, and limits live display projection to five minutes of mutually
  fresh evidence.
- Retries collection after service recovery, connectivity changes, reboot,
  app replacement, sign-in, and permission or consent changes. A network
  transition is only a retry signal; Wi-Fi identity never leaves the phone and
  is never proximity proof.
- Adds a dashboard, per-phone Fix counting view, coordinate-free daily
  timeline, and audited correction flow. Day boundaries use the couple's home
  IANA timezone and support 23- and 25-hour daylight-saving days.
- Offers optional, latest-only collection-health sharing with the active
  partner. It is disabled by default, expires within 24 hours, is purged on
  opt-out or unpair, and is excluded from backups and administration.

## Our Space

- Removes manual Save and uses a single serialized autosave path after 800 ms
  idle, at least every five seconds during continuous typing, and during safe
  lifecycle exits.
- Persists one stable creation identity before the first request and adopts the
  returned note ID within that same workspace, preventing repeated autosave or
  a lost response from creating another document.
- Stops on a newer shared revision and offers Review and merge, Save mine as a
  copy, or Use shared version. No offline draft overwrites a partner's newer
  revision automatically.
- Forks clean source-owned attachments with quota admission, digest checks, and
  rewritten Markdown IDs. Missing or foreign mappings fail closed.
- Restores animated GIF playback after sanitization, includes a visible
  pause/play control, and starts paused when Android reduced motion is active.

## Deployment safety

Operations performs a dry-run duplicate classification, creates and verifies
an encrypted backup, upgrades the schema, reaggregates the retained 30-day
coordinate-free history, and applies only safe duplicate archival. Historical
gaps without retained evidence are not manufactured. Ambiguous duplicate
groups remain for manual review. The owner waived the unavailable physical
tablet soak for this release; that check remains recorded as unperformed.
