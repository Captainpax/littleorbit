# Little Orbit 1.1.1

Little Orbit 1.1.1 was published at `2026-09-19T23:45:53.67375Z` as an optional watch-reliability patch. It keeps the phone compatibility floor at code 23 and has no forced-update deadline.

## Changes

- Makes Wear Smooch queue insertion mutable and preserves a five-item encrypted bound, stable operation IDs, duplicate handling, 15-minute expiry, retries, and acknowledgements.
- Purges expired and impossible-future queue records and prevents rapid taps or activity recreation from inventing duplicate sends.
- Moves Together Time diagnostic queue counts off the Android main thread and ignores callbacks from stopped or superseded activity loads.
- Binds each active Wear relationship generation to the phone node that delivered its first valid managed record. Other nodes cannot receive or mutate watch action state.
- Uses receipt time for phone-side freshness, clamps queue counts, and resolves the local Wear node once per sync batch.
- Refreshes Watch settings, the five-stage installer, and round-watch destinations against the three 1.1.1 concept sheets.
- Adds isolated phone/Wear smoke variants and a lower-version fixture for side-by-side installer QA without modifying production.
