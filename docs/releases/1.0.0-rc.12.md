# Little Orbit 1.0.0-rc.12

RC12 gives the Android app a complete responsive navigation shell, makes Our Space attachments and Markdown controls calmer and more reliable, and adds a private recent-activity view for couples.

## Changes

- Replaces fixed bottom tabs with a complete left navigation drawer on phones and a persistent, non-modal rail on screens 840dp or wider.
- Adds a right contextual panel whose actions follow Home, Quiz, Smooches, Our Space, Countdowns, Together time, Profile, and Privacy.
- Adds a current-couple activity timeline for note, attachment, countdown, quiz, and Smooch events. The timeline stores no note body, attachment name, quiz answer, location, email, or free-form message, expires after 30 days, and disappears on unpairing.
- Moves Our Space formatting into a compact dock above the keyboard, with common Markdown actions immediately available and structures such as quotes, code, tables, and dividers in an overflow menu.
- Waits for attachment scanning and sanitization before inserting a Markdown reference. Available cards expose Insert, Preview, Delete, and Keep offline; rejected cards explain the failure and offer Choose again or Remove.
- Repairs transparent PNG and animated GIF sanitization, preserves bounded animation timing, and rechecks the final sanitized size and couple quota under lock before making bytes available.
- Adds a disposable paired-account Compose stack and repeatable Android smoke workflow isolated from hosted accounts, volumes, ports, and SMTP credentials.
- Repairs API 29 cutout compatibility and API 30 Material theme inheritance found by the emulator matrix.
