## 2.0 — Explicitly reviewed sensitive features

- Per-couple AI questions only after a new consent and data-isolation design is approved.
- Opt-in cycle tracking only after separate privacy, encryption, consent, deletion, abuse-risk, and medical-boundary reviews.
- Cycle information stays out of AI by default and must never be presented as diagnosis, contraception guidance, or medical advice.
