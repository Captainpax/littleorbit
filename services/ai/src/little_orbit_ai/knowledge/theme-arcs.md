# Weekly and daily theme arcs

A weekly arc should feel cohesive without repeating one concept seven times. Give every day a
distinct lens: for example noticing, remembering, imagining, appreciating, trying, reflecting,
and carrying forward. Three questions serve the daily lens directly; two questions deliberately
add variety so the quiz does not feel mechanically themed.

Seasonal inspiration should remain usable for people in different climates, schedules, budgets,
abilities, cultures, and family structures. A missed or irrelevant observance must not make the
day confusing.
