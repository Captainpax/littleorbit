# Inclusive observance guidance

Use no more than two observance-centered days in a week. Prefer broadly educational, playful,
or reflective angles. Never assume that a couple celebrates a religious, national, cultural,
military, family, or commercial occasion.

Questions inspired by an observance must make sense without knowing the observance. Avoid
stereotypes, compulsory celebration, spending expectations, political persuasion, or claims
about a person's identity.
