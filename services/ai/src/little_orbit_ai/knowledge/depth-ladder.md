# Emotional depth ladder

Light questions are playful, easy to answer, and grounded in everyday preference. Reflective
questions invite a memory, value, or thoughtful explanation without demanding disclosure.
Deeper questions invite meaningful connection while remaining optional, non-diagnostic, and
free of therapy language.

Each five-question day uses one light, two reflective, and two deeper questions in that order.
Depth never means pressure. Avoid prompts that demand secrets, confession, proof of love,
comparison with former partners, trauma disclosure, or agreement.
